Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

1. Overview

Jayasree Designs jewellery is handcrafted in small batches, and many pieces are made to order. This policy explains when an order can be cancelled, how refunds are processed, and which items cannot be returned.

2. Cancellation window

  • Orders may be cancelled within 12 hours of order confirmation on WhatsApp, or before production starts on a made-to-order piece — whichever is sooner.
  • To cancel, message the same WhatsApp number used to place the order with your name and order reference.
  • Once a made-to-order piece has entered production, cancellation is declined.
  • Orders already handed to the courier cannot be cancelled; see the returns process below.

3. Returns & replacements

  • Only damaged, faulty, or incorrect items qualify for return — change-of-mind returns are not accepted.
  • If a piece arrives damaged, faulty, or incorrect, notify Jayasree Designs within 48 hours of delivery with a mandatory unboxing video and photos of the item and packaging.
  • Approved returns must be sent back within 5 days, unworn and in original packaging.
  • Return shipping is paid by Jayasree Designs, only for damaged or incorrect items.
  • Where a replacement is not possible, a refund will be issued per section 4.

4. Refund processing timeline

  • Once a cancellation or return is approved, refunds are initiated within 3–5 business days.
  • Refunds are made to the original payment method used for the order.
  • After initiation, it typically takes a further 5–7 business days for the amount to reflect, depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • Original shipping charges are non-refundable, except where the item was damaged or incorrect.

5. Non-returnable items

  • Custom, personalised, or made-to-order pieces.
  • Sale, clearance, or discounted items marked "final sale".
  • Items showing signs of wear, damage after delivery, or missing original packaging.
  • Gift-with-purchase or free promotional items.
