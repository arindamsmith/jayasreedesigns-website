Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

1. Where we ship

Jayasree Designs currently ships across West Bengal. Pan-India and international shipping is not available yet.

2. Order processing & dispatch days

  • We dispatch orders regularly throughout the week. Once your payment is confirmed, we'll share the exact dispatch date for your order on WhatsApp.
  • Made-to-order pieces: crafted and dispatched within 7-10 business days; the exact date is confirmed on WhatsApp.
  • We do not process or dispatch orders during major festival holidays (e.g. Durga Puja); orders placed during this period are processed once operations resume.
  • A tracking ID is shared on WhatsApp once the parcel is handed to the courier.

3. Couriers

Shipments are sent via our courier partner, selected based on the destination pin code and serviceability.

4. Expected delivery windows

Estimated transit time after dispatch (business days):

  • Kolkata: 1–2 days
  • Rest of West Bengal: 2–4 days

These are courier estimates, not guarantees. Weather, strikes, regional restrictions and peak-season volumes can add delay.

5. Shipping charges

Shipping charges are confirmed on WhatsApp before payment is requested.

6. Packaging

Each piece is wrapped in protective material and sent in a tamper-evident pouch or box. We recommend recording an unboxing video — it is required for any damage or missing-item claim (see the Refund & Cancellation Policy).

7. Delays, wrong address & failed delivery

  • Please double-check your address and phone number before confirming — the studio is not responsible for delays or loss due to an incorrect or incomplete address.
  • If a parcel is returned to us due to a failed delivery or unclaimed shipment, re-dispatch will incur a further shipping charge.
  • For parcels stuck in transit beyond the estimated window, message us and we will raise a query with the courier.
